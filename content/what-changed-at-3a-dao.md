3A DAO ran a lending protocol and a euro stablecoin, EURO3, on Polygon and Linea. On 30 September 2024 we announced
that both would be phased out, because the protocol and EURO3 had not reached the adoption and use we needed.

## The phase-out, step by step

- **1 October 2024:** no new EURO3 loans, no new collateral, and A3A Stability Pool rewards stopped.
- **14 October 2024:** all vaults opened for redemption.
- **30 November 2024:** all remaining vaults closed, the EURO3 liquidity controlled by 3A DAO was withdrawn from
  exchanges, and the lending web app was discontinued.

Source: [Phasing out EURO3 lending](https://medium.com/@3aDAO/phasing-out-euro3-lending-f5869a3498a8), 3A DAO's
announcement of 30 September 2024.

## If you find an old post or page

Our X account, our earlier blog on Medium and the old protocol documentation describe the lending protocol as it was.
They are kept as history, not as a product you can use.

The old documentation now lives at [docs.3adao.org/3a-protocol](https://docs.3adao.org/3a-protocol), and every page
there carries an "Archived" notice. If a page or a post gives you a lending app link or a EURO3 contract address, do
not send funds to it.
